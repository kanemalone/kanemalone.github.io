How Malone scores.

The scoring engine is deliberately selective. This page documents the mathematics, the context layers and the live model coverage without changing the underlying scoring system.

HIGH-LEVEL SCORE

Growth35%
Quality25%
Momentum15%
Analyst signal25%

High-Level is the broad market-facing signal used alongside Deep Score in the legacy verdict framework.

DEEP SCORE

Growth25%
Quality20%
Value15%
Cash Flow15%
Revisions10%
Momentum5%
Dividend10%

OPPORTUNITY

Opportunity = 60% Deep + 25% Story + 15% Upside Score

Opportunity is a context layer. It does not replace the legacy Deep/High-Level verdict.

MALONE FAIR VALUE

Expected Upside = (Malone Fair Value ÷ Current Price − 1) × 100

Fair value v2 blends guarded forward-EPS valuation, a positive-FCF cross-check and analyst mean target. Extreme growth inputs are excluded from the valuation multiple.

VERDICT RULES

BUYDeep ≥82 and High-Level ≥75
WATCHDeep ≥72 and High-Level ≥65
HOLDDeep ≥60
AVOIDOtherwise

BUY is downgraded to WATCH if Value <45. A stock is forced to AVOID if Quality <35 and Cash Flow <35. ETFs remain ETF.
