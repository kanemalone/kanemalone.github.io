MALONE SCORE V6.0

One score.
One decision.

A simpler investor-facing model. Seven independent factors produce a 0–100 Malone Score, then the valuation and risk gates determine BUY, HOLD or SELL.

The seven factors

Growth20%
Revenue, earnings, forward EPS, forward revenue and long-term growth.
Quality20%
Margins, ROE, ROIC and ROA.
Financial Strength15%
Cash generation, FCF yield, leverage and payout quality.
Valuation20%
Forward/trailing multiples, PEG, EV/EBITDA, P/B, FCF yield and Malone Fair Value.
Momentum10%
52-week range position rather than simply distance from the high.
Earnings Revisions10%
Estimate revisions, analyst actions, EPS trend and earnings surprise.
Dividend / Shareholder Return5%
Yield and payout discipline without penalising reinvestment-heavy growth companies.

Headline calculation

Malone Score = Growth × 20% + Quality × 20% + Financial × 15% + Valuation × 20% + Momentum × 10% + Revisions × 10% + Dividend × 5%

Components are rounded first, then the headline score is calculated from those displayed values. This makes the model auditable.

Decision engine

BUY
Score ≥ 70
Quality ≥ 45 · Financial ≥ 40 · Valuation ≥ 45
HOLD
45–69
Good enough to own, but not enough risk/reward to buy aggressively.
SELL
Score < 45
Or severe quality + financial weakness.

A high-quality company can still be HOLD if valuation is unattractive. Cheap alone is not enough for BUY.
