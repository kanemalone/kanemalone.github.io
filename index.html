MALONE FRAMEWORK v4.2

High-Level: Growth 35%, Quality 25%, Momentum 15%, Analyst signal 25%.

Deep: Growth 25%, Quality 20%, Value 15%, Cash Flow 15%, Revisions 10%, Momentum 5%, Dividend 10%.

Screening: Market, market-cap size, sector, verdict, asset type, style and score filters are separate from the scoring engine.

Market-cap bands: Mega ≥$200B; Large $10B–$200B; Mid $2B–$10B; Small $300M–$2B; Micro <$300M. Market cap is converted to a common USD-style bucket using the data returned by Yahoo Finance.

Verdict: BUY requires Deep ≥82 and High-Level ≥75. WATCH requires Deep ≥72 and High-Level ≥65. HOLD requires Deep ≥60. Otherwise AVOID. A BUY is downgraded to WATCH if Value <45. A stock is forced to AVOID if Quality <35 and Cash Flow <35. ETFs remain labelled ETF.

This is a screening model, not financial advice. Missing metrics are excluded from individual factor averages rather than treated as zero.
