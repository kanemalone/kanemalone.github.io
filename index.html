MALONE FRAMEWORK v4.4.3

High-Level: Growth 35%, Quality 25%, Momentum 15%, Analyst signal 25%.

Deep: Growth 25%, Quality 20%, Value 15%, Cash Flow 15%, Revisions 10%, Momentum 5%, Dividend 10%.

Story: A forward-looking quantitative proxy using growth runway, revisions, earnings delivery, analyst upside/conviction and investment-phase context. It does not replace fundamental scores.

Opportunity: 60% Deep + 25% Story + 15% valuation-upside score. This is a context layer; the legacy Deep/High-Level verdict remains visible.

Fair value v2: Malone blends a guarded forward-EPS valuation, positive-FCF cross-check and analyst mean target. Extreme growth inputs are excluded from the valuation multiple, valuation anchors are constrained to avoid one outlier dominating, and the upside score is deliberately saturating. Bear/base/bull are a ±15% range around Malone fair value. It is an estimate, not a guaranteed price target.

Investment context: when FCF is weak but operating cash flow is positive and R&D/capex intensity is meaningful, Malone flags deliberate reinvestment rather than treating all weak FCF as identical.

Monthly snapshots: the latest valuation is refreshed with the data run and one monthly record is retained per symbol, allowing fair value, upside, Story and Opportunity to be compared over time.

Screening: Market, market-cap size, sector, industry, verdict, asset type, style, score, Story, expected upside, Opportunity and quick-ranking filters are separate from the core Deep mathematics.

Market-cap bands: Mega ≥$200B; Large $10B–$200B; Mid $2B–$10B; Small $300M–$2B; Micro <$300M. Market cap is converted to a common USD-style bucket using Yahoo Finance data.

Legacy verdict: BUY requires Deep ≥82 and High-Level ≥75. WATCH requires Deep ≥72 and High-Level ≥65. HOLD requires Deep ≥60. Otherwise AVOID. A BUY is downgraded to WATCH if Value <45. A stock is forced to AVOID if Quality <35 and Cash Flow <35. ETFs remain labelled ETF.

This is a screening model, not financial advice. Missing metrics are excluded from individual factor averages rather than treated as zero.
