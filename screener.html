MALONE FRAMEWORK V6.0

Malone Score: a single 0–100 score built from seven independent factors.

Weights: Growth 20%, Quality 20%, Financial Strength 15%, Valuation 20%, Momentum 10%, Earnings Revisions 10%, Dividend / Shareholder Return 5%.

Decision: BUY requires Malone Score ≥70 plus Quality ≥45, Financial Strength ≥40 and Valuation ≥45. SELL is below 45 or triggered by severe quality + financial weakness. Everything else is HOLD.

Fair Value: Malone blends guarded forward-EPS valuation, positive-FCF cross-check and analyst mean target. Extreme growth inputs are excluded from the valuation multiple and anchors are constrained so one outlier cannot dominate.

Momentum: uses position inside the 52-week range rather than simply price divided by the 52-week high.

Financial Strength: no automatic score is awarded just because FCF or operating cash flow exists; cash generation, FCF yield, leverage and payout quality are considered.

Monthly snapshots: valuation, score and decision history are retained so changes can be explained over time.

Market data: the 1,000-stock universe is ASX 500 + US 500 and the Market homepage also receives major index, volatility, rates, FX and commodity data.

This is a screening model, not financial advice. Missing metrics are excluded from individual factor averages rather than treated as zero.
